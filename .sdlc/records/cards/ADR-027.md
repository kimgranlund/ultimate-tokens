# ADR-027 · A seat cites only what it measured, at the ref it is writing about · 2026-09-22 · PROPOSED
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Decision | Four rules, binding on every seat that writes a record under `.sdlc/` or a review, verdict, brief or handoff anywhere in the repo. (1) A seat that cites a figure or a state is the seat that measured it, at the ref the record names, by a command the record shows, or cites the sha it read instead of asserting. (2) A summary of a measurement is a lead, never evidence: the seat reruns before it relies. (3) A constraint inherited from another seat is interrogated before it is obeyed: the record states why it exists, or files the question. (4) An acceptance criterion rewritten after its verdict must be re-graded by a verifier before the unit closes (source: #709 revision 38). |
| Forces | Over one review round of #681 U5, four defects arose from three seats through one mechanism, a figure or judgement carried forward from a summary of a measurement rather than the measurement; a fifth followed at a preserved historical line read at the wrong ref. A second form: a constraint recorded and obeyed with nobody asking why it existed (the `56 to 60 s` prefix warning and #718 are one sentence read from two ends). Records-followup revision 37 moved a unit's acceptance after its grade, written by the seat being graded. |
| Rejected | none named |
| Consequences | A verdict or review row carries the command and its printed output at the sha the record names. A brief, scope addendum or recon keeps the summarised seat's caveats or cites its finding by path and sha. A constraint without a reason becomes a question, not a rule. No new gate is proposed: what a check can enforce is #723's work. #718 and #719 stand as the two recorded instances. |
