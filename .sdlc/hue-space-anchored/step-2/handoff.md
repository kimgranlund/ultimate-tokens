   - error `err(h)`: `cam16FromRgb(renderAt(h)).hue - targetCam16Hue`, wrapped to (-180, 180]; a candidate whose `cam16FromRgb(renderAt(h)).chroma` is below 5 is achromatic and carries no signal (CAM16 hue is noise below C 5, the floor `test/engine/anchor.mjs` `ACHROMATIC_CELL_C` already uses).
   - fast path: `h <- h - err(h)` from the seed, at most 16 iterations, return at `|err| < 1e-3`; an achromatic candidate breaks to the scan.
   - scan: offsets -60 to 60 in steps of 5 around the seed; a bracket is two adjacent non-achromatic samples whose errors change sign with `|err difference| < 90` (that excludes the +-180 wrap); take the bracket whose midpoint is nearest the seed and bisect at most 40 times to a width of 1e-4; an achromatic midpoint returns the seed.
   - no bracket: return the seed (the anchor's OKLCH hue), never a least-error candidate, which on ill-conditioned pale stops invents a tint (architect Carry forward: 3,210 pale stop-solves have no bracketed root).
