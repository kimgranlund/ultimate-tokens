# ADR-008 · OKLCH→CAM16 hue is a sampled mapping · 2026-07-12 · SUPERSEDED (by ADR-011)
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Decision | Map an OKLCH hue to CAM16 by sampling one fixed mid color (L=0.72, C=0.10) at that OKLCH hue and reading its CAM16 angle. Memoized. |
| Forces | Users may enter hues in OKLCH while the engine works in CAM16 hue, CAM16 hue at a given OKLCH hue varies with L and C, an exact per-color mapping is not well-defined for a single "hue input", the fixed sample is a deliberate reproducible compromise |
| Rejected | none named |
| Consequences | A few degrees of drift vs. an exact mapping. Acceptable for hue input; not used for output color math. Superseding note: the fixed mid-sample mapping (and its "few degrees of drift") is gone; `oklchToCam16Hue` is now a chroma-aware Newton inverse that lands the rendered identity color on the stored OKLCH hue to ~0.00°. |
