# SITE-licensing · Licensing identity — email-bound licenses (decision record + plan) · 2026-07-12 · none stated (document header says "Decided 2026-07-02")
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Decision | The unit of license enforcement is the email, not the device: a license unlocks any number of computers and Figma installs as long as the person's email matches the key's associated email; device/activation counts stop being the seat model and remain only as an abuse backstop. Phase 1 (interim, client-only): set `license_activation_limit` high (e.g. 25) for Pro and Studio-per-seat alike, and the Account panel asks for email + license key, comparing the entered email to `meta.customer_email` (case-insensitive, trimmed), with mismatch rejected as an anomaly (fail-closed, never `revoked`). Phase 2 (rides mcp-hosting Phase B): magic-link-verified account email becomes the identity, LS webhooks upsert `license ↔ email` into D1, Studio becomes named seats (a seat = a person/email, 5 included, +$19/seat/year, devices unlimited per person), and the Figma plugin stays offline and free with identity never entering it. Comms: no support inbox and no sending domain; support is GitHub Issues, Lemon Squeezy sends all transactional mail, and `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}` is pinned to `<SUPPORT_EMAIL>` across the corpus. |
| Forces | Lemon Squeezy already associates email with license at purchase (`meta.customer_email` on validate/activate, `user_email` on license-keys/orders/subscriptions, webhooks), the product is unattributed and owns no domain, the Figma plugin must stay offline, Phase 1 proves knowledge of the purchase email rather than inbox ownership (claim discipline: copy may not say "secure" or "verified" until Phase 2) |
| Rejected | none named |
| Consequences | Phase 1 code touchpoints: `src/engine/flags.js` (`lemonActivation`/`lemonEntitlement` gain an `email` opt + `emailMatch` check), `src/main.ts` (`_licenseService` passes the email through), `src/ui/app.js` (Account panel email field). Shipped behavior (#131) stays as-is until Phase 1 lands (device activation consumes an activation; `enterLicense`→activate, `clearLicense`→deactivate). Customer copy keeps describing shipped behavior until Phase 1 ships, then moves to "tied to your email, works on all your devices" via the `ultimate-tokens-brand-voice` loop. Go-live (#158) does not wait for this; only the runbook's activation-limit guidance changes. Lifecycle mail from Phase B needs a `<SUPPORT_EMAIL>` on a domain that does not yet exist, an explicit prerequisite. Related records: `mcp-hosting-spec.md`, `storage-and-sync-spec.md`, `docs/marketing/store-copy.md` §3. |
