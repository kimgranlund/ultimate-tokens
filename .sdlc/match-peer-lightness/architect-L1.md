## Approach

Two layers of one idea, both built on a palette-free CIELAB L* ladder `sharedToneAt(stop, controls)` in `src/engine/tonal.js` (even: `toneAt(stop, 0, 0, {curve, lmin, lmax, tension})`; perceptual and peak: the neutral-grey L* of `lerp(okhslLAt(lmax), okhslLAt(lmin), (stop-50)/900)`), and one shared tint rule, chroma = `chromaFloor/100 x maxChromaInGamut(hue, L*)` (the existing "% of each stop's gamut ceiling" control, promoted from even-only to every tone mode). The ramp layer runs once per palette with no peer access (`src/engine/layers.mjs:147-155`), so both rules are per-palette targets, never peer-derived.

**(1) `ramp@2`, the default extremes rule.** Today's `paletteStops` is frozen verbatim as `src/engine/layers/ramp@1.mjs` (a copy of `tonal.js` exporting `paletteStops`, importing `hct.js`/`okhsl.js` only; SHA-256 in `FROZEN.json`, checked by `test/engine/layers.mjs` c4.4), registered in `REGISTRY` at version 1; the live `tonal.js` becomes `ramp@2`; `layer-pins.mjs` `LATEST.ramp = 2`. A stored document keeps `layers.ramp: 1` (the v10 stamp, `persist.js:453`) and renders byte-identically; a new document (`pinLatest`) and every preset (`persist.js:778`) take 2. No schema bump is needed for the pin itself. Construction, every tone mode, anchored or not, at palette chroma 100 (the group damper `dampStops` scales the result by Base chroma afterwards, so a neutral palette's ends stay near-grey): the end bands are stops 050..100 and 900..950 (Radix steps 1 and 12 in both schemes). In a band, tone = `sharedToneAt(stop)` and chroma = the tint rule at that L* (050 stays white: maxc 0). Between 500 and a band edge the palette's own construction is kept but affinely remapped per side so that stop 100 (light) and 900 (dark) land exactly on `sharedToneAt(100)` / `sharedToneAt(900)`: `anchorLerp` (`tonal.js:746`) generalised to knots `[pivot at 500, band edge at 100/900]` instead of `[pivot, lmax at 050 / lmin at 950]`, mapping the unit read `t` over `[t(100), t500]` onto `[shared(100), pivot]`. An affine remap of a monotone function is monotone (anchorLerp's own proof), the pieces meet exactly at 100/900, and the band ladder is monotone, so the whole ramp is monotone by construction; stop 500 stays the verbatim anchor (ADR-026 holds, `anchor-ramp` C3 holds). Chroma blends from the palette's own value at 500 to the tint value at the band edge with the `anchorChromaBasis` smoothstep (`:644`) re-keyed to reach 1 at 100/900, then follows the tint rule inside the band; no notch at 500 (weight 0 there). On the OKHSL paths the band stops solve `l` for the shared L* (`solveLForTone`, `:1149`) and `s` for the target chroma at that held L* (new `solveSForChroma`, bisection); on the even path HCT tone is L* directly. Unanchored palettes use their own `lightnessAt(500, t)` as the pivot. Measured today (344 docs, 3,796 palettes): stop 100 L* spread median 4.6 max 15.2, stop 900 median 6.1, stop 950 median 0.9, tint-fraction spread 0.13 to 0.37; after ramp@2 the band spread is the shared ladder's own (0.2 to 0.4 L* in even, within 8-bit rounding elsewhere).

**(2) The opt-in mode on top.** `matchPeerLightness` (boolean, default false, read by `ramp@2` only) moves the band edge from 100/900 to 500: the whole ramp is `sharedToneAt`, stop 500 is no longer the anchor pixel (built like the `clamped` case, `:881`, `:1418`, at the anchor's hue and chroma basis), skew/lift stop moving lightness (lift keeps its chroma-envelope role), vibrancy/cusp pull are `t = 0`, peak keeps only its chroma cap (ceiling from `preCap(500).chroma`, `:1471`). One parameter, `bandEdge(controls) = match ? 500 : 100`, is the entire composition. `ramp@1` never reads the flag, so the inspector shows the control only when `doc.layers.ramp === 2` and otherwise offers "upgrade to latest" (ADR-034 §3; no such affordance exists today, grep `upgrade` in `src/ui`), which re-pins and re-renders.

**(3) What moves for `ramp@2`.** Fixtures: `test/engine/fixtures/tonal-legacy.json` (regen `scripts/gen-tonal-fixture.mjs`), `mode-isolation.json` (fingerprints at `mode-isolation-gate.mjs:34`), `shadcn-baseline.css` and `radix-baseline.json` (`test/engine/exports.mjs:361`), the Adia derived exports (`scripts/gen-adia-derived-exports.mjs`), `src/ui/describe-mcp-assets.js`. Gates: `ramp-identity` / `scripts/report-preset-fidelity.mjs` reports nonzero by design, the plan declares the movement and the verdict copies the six `identity <mode>` lines (`.sdlc/adapter.md:39`); `report-compute-neutral.mjs` must read 0 cells for a `ramp@1`-pinned subject (the default kit re-pinned to 1 under `--only default-kit`) and the preset delta is the declared change; Gate A basis (`chroma-envelope-gate.mjs`, the curve spec against the band stops), `even-dips-gate.mjs` (the even floor law no longer governs the band), `curated-contrast.mjs --full` counts (050/950 are on-colour ends; the 4.5:1 fall-through may flip for a few families), `anchor.mjs` C5 monotone/gap/distinct allow-lists (recounted, C2/C3 unchanged), `tonal.mjs` `white-endpoint`, `lift-monotonic`, `skew-lift-okhsl` named exceptions. `test/engine/layers.mjs` and `layer-pins.mjs` gain the first real frozen version (`LATEST` must equal `latestOf(REGISTRY)`). Exports stamp `ramp@2` in JSON/DTCG/UI3 (`exports.js:440`, `:598`, `:665`); `EXPORT_SCHEMA_VERSION` 7 stays (the stamp format is unchanged). Records: ADR-035 (ramp@2, the band rule, the frozen-copy rule: a frozen ramp depends on `hct.js`/`okhsl.js`, which are gated separately and may not change output), knowledge-02 §9, color-math SKILL line 42, changelog, plus the before/after board (`docs/reports/<date>-ramp2-extremes.md` with smoke screenshots) shown to the user before landing.

## Constraints and assumptions

- Per-palette ramp layer, no peers: verified `layers.mjs:147-155`.
- Presets re-pin to latest on open, stored docs keep pins: verified `persist.js:778`, `:453-458`, `layer-pins.mjs:20-26`.
- Affine remap with band-edge knots is monotone and continuous: verified from `anchorLerp`'s construction `:746-756` | numeric gap (0.55 L*) and distinct checks are the builder's (`anchor.mjs` C5).
- `dampStops` scales band chroma by Base chroma so neutrals stay grey: verified `:992`, `:1590-1604`.
- No surface serializes the resolved controls: verified `exports.js:441`, `ds-export.js`.
- Frozen `ramp@1` importing `hct.js`/`okhsl.js` keeps byte identity: unverified; holds only while those modules' outputs are unchanged (their gates: `test/engine/hct.mjs`, `okhsl.mjs`).
- Chroma floor 40 reads as "faint" at the near-white end: unverified; the board decides, the user can lower it.

## Rejected alternatives

- The extremes rule inside `ramp@1` as a default change: moves every stored kit silently, ADR-034 §3 forbids.
- Value-domain blending (`lerp(ownL, sharedL, w)`) between 500 and the band: not monotone by construction when the shared ladder is lighter than the own one on the dark side; needs `enforceMonotonePixelL` as a crutch.
- Peer-derived shared pivot: breaks the per-palette layer shape.
- A new "end tint" control: duplicates `chromaFloor`'s meaning.
- A shared absolute chroma at the ends: reads stronger on thin-gamut hues; the gamut fraction is the perceptual measure.
- Copying `hct.js`/`okhsl.js` into the frozen module: 2,000 more frozen lines for a dependency already output-gated.

## Risks

- A band that starts at 100 while the anchor sits at L* 33 (Warning) compresses 100 to 500 into the light side's remap: steeper interior steps, possible sub-0.55 L* gaps near 100 or 900 on dark or light anchors (C5 recount; if an allow-list grows, that is a declared cost, not a hide).
- Corpus contrast: a few families' on-colour fall-through may flip at 050/950; the full sweep is the measurement, counts re-baselined with a reason.
- Chroma floor at 40 may be a stronger tint than "faint"; the before/after board is the check, and lowering the default moves even-mode interiors too.
- Near-black quantization may exceed the 0.15 fraction tolerance for some hues; measured, then ruled.
- First real frozen layer: `report-compute-neutral` renders presets at latest, so its "0 differing cells" now applies only to ramp@1-pinned subjects; the tool needs a `--pin ramp=1` leg or the plan reads the preset delta as the declared change.
- No upgrade affordance exists; stored kits stay on ramp@1 until one ships.

Cheapest ordered steps: (1) freeze `ramp@1.mjs` + `FROZEN.json` + registry/pins, with `peer-lightness.mjs` as the red gate; (2) `sharedToneAt`, band-edge `anchorLerp`, `solveSForChroma`, the band rule in the even paths; (3) the band rule in the OKHSL paths and `holdTone` target; (4) regenerate fixtures, recount allow-lists, declare the movement, neutrality legs; (5) the opt-in flag: persist v11, model, controls input, the mode branch (`bandEdge = 500`), gates for the whole ramp; (6) inspector control, slider gating, headless and smoke; (7) ADR-035, docs, changelog, the before/after board for the user.
