## Root cause

Four false sentences and six imprecise ones reached a PASS review because nothing in the unit's contract could fail on a false sentence.

1. The criteria were structural, not factual. U3's criteria were C1, C10 and C11: the test run is green, the `docs/` diff is a subset of ten named paths, seven greps on two test files print fixed counts. A records unit graded on which files moved and which identifiers are absent cannot fail on what the moved files say. The plan wrote every engine criterion as command, exact Expected, negative control, Today, and then handed the unit whose whole output is prose no criterion of that shape at all.
2. The plan itself carried two of the false claims, and the builder copied them. The U3 line told the builder to write "the even floor is envelope-relative in the interior" (revision 14 changed the floor to `chromaFloor% * min(maxc, floorRef)`, which has no envelope term, and never repaired the U2 heading or the U3 line that still say envelope-relative), "both allow-lists retired" (C11 and the U1 text say three) and "a dated amendment line under ADR-025" (the on-color ADR; ADR-026's Consequences line 767 is where #701's `chromaFloor` side is recorded). F6 and F9 are the plan's defects surfaced through the builder; the builder flagged the ADR placement in the handoff and the review graded it Low because the plan said so.
3. The ruling that governs the even-dips row arrived the same day and lives in an untracked file. R65 (2026-09-28) is recorded in `.sdlc/runtime/owner-rulings-2026-09-22.md`, which `.gitignore:15` ignores, and in `.sdlc/questions/chroma-floor-R57-even-dips.md` on `origin/main` @ 537de9db, which is not on the plan branch. The builder cited R57 (the mode-isolation ruling) and the ignored runtime file for both rows, so the branch names a ruling the reader cannot follow and omits the one that applies. The handoff's `Host` row cites the same ignored path.
4. Derived figures were written next to their derivation and not re-derived. The adapter's `sweeps` row states its sum and its seven addends in the same cell; U3 changed two addends (mode-isolation `20 to 34` became `48 to 65`, even-dips `26 to 33` became `19 to 23`) and left the sum. A node one-liner over the adapter's own rows prints `376 to 496 (86+79+67+57+20+48+19 to 116+100+86+83+23+65+23)` against the row's `355 to 475`. The same pattern made F4 and F5: the CHANGELOG summarises U2's log by rounding its meaning, "90 named dips. Both are 0 now" where 58 (57 at 450, 1 at 550) are 0 and 32 at stop 500 remain (the entry's own line 19 says so), and "one tertiary ramp" where U2's handoff line 106 reads `max dC overall | 23.38, Tongass secondary stop 175`.
5. The review verified formulas, not sentences. Its "Docs true of the engine" row checks that the formula strings match `tonal.js` (they do) and stops there; the prose around them (envelope-relative, the whole baseline credited to the old floor, "nothing beyond 400/600 moves", "0.2 lifted-stop units") was not read against the code, the sum was not recomputed, and the ruling name was taken from the baseline row rather than from the ruling. A reviewer-l1 on a records unit confirmed what the builder quoted instead of re-deriving it from the sources.

So the defect is the unit's contract, not the builder's care alone: a records unit needs a criterion whose command reads the record against its source and reds on a mismatch. Revision 17 adds that criterion (C14) and repairs the three plan sentences that seeded F6 and F9.
