1. 🟡 C2.8 authority. `.sdlc/questions/pane-context-schema7.md` on main has `Default if unanswered | A` and no owner Answer. The schema-7 re-pin is mechanically right (head `1`/`0`, control `0`/`1`), but the authority is a default, not an owner answer. Land needs the owner to answer or accept the default.

Two existing lines were changed in place: the #766 amendment's chroma-envelope sentence gained `under #766 alone; with the damper merged under it the committed fixture holds 500`, and the ADR-026 Quick map row was extended. The Quick map change is in the plan's lane. The amendment edit is a rewrite of history text. If the record is append-only, move that clause into the new amendment.
4. 🟡 The C2.6 negative control as written does not bite. `r = g / 90` gave `0 cells rose` twice: once as written, once with the `Math.min(1, ...)` clamp also removed. Two things stop it. `groupDamper` clamps r to 1, and a group-100 subject never enters `dampStops`, because the `palette.chroma !== 100` guard skips it. So the plan's rationale "a group-100 subject renders above its old value" is false. Only an unclamped `g / 40` reds, and it reds by `1 cells rose` (even 300 p90). The `--compare` is a thin sentinel for amplification. The plan's control text needs correcting.
5. 🟡 Low. With the dark example wrapper dropped, the shim throws `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'querySelectorAll')` at headless-boot.mjs:618 instead of printing a labelled `✗ (k1b...)`. It is still rc 1, so the gate holds, but the failure is not self-describing.
6. 🟢 Visible effect for old saved documents. This is ruled (R98: no migration) and recorded here so the owner sees the size. On the stripped ramp-identity leg, a default-kit doc saved with material 30 moves Neutral by up to `max dL* 21.04` on peak and `4.07` on perceptual. Across the corpus it is 358 of 3780 palettes. The authored leg is small (peak max dL* `0.17`).
