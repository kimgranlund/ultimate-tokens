## Diagnosis: three mechanisms and one coupling; two fixed by construction, the third re-ruled (R74)

**Mechanism 1, READING (b): the ruled multiplier bars are unreachable at the shipped constants, by closed form.** `chromaEnvelope` (`src/engine/tonal.js:430`) returns `max(0, 1 + shoulder - (damp/100) * |sd|^dampCurve)` with `sd = (liftStop(stop) - liftStop(500)) / 450`, so at lift 0 stop 300 sits at |sd| = 4/9 and stop 100 at |sd| = 8/9. At `damp` 70 and `dampCurve` 1.5 that is 0.793 and 0.413 for every palette, the 79.3 and 41.3 the report prints. The bars ask 0.75 and 0.25 at those two abscissae, two equations in the two constants `d = damp/100` and `c = dampCurve`:

```
d * (4/9)^c = 0.25        (stop 300 at exactly the bar)
d * (8/9)^c = 0.75        (stop 100 at exactly the bar)
divide:  2^c = 3   =>   c = log2(3) = 1.58496
then:    d = 0.25 / (4/9)^1.58496 = 0.9045   (damp 90.4)
```

The pair is unique: the ratio of the two residues fixes `c` alone, and `d` follows. Measured through the report's own loop (lift and skew vary the abscissae per palette, so the corpus median is the check, not the algebra): `damp` 91, `dampCurve` 1.585 prints READING (b) 74.8 / 24.5, PASS in both modes; `damp` 92 prints 74.6 / 23.6, the one-point margin this plan adopts so the stop-300 p90 (which sits exactly on 90.0 at 91, see below) clears with room. Even mode takes its own branch of the same function (`EVEN_DAMP_FACTOR` 0.25 on the residue, `EVEN_NEIGHBOURHOOD_R`, `tonal.js:403` to 445) and #701 owns it: the retune is scoped to the perceptual/peak branch by a mode-scoped constant of the same shape as `EVEN_DAMP_FACTOR` (the residue form `100 - (100 - damp) * OKHSL_DAMP_RESIDUE` with `OKHSL_DAMP_RESIDUE = 0.27` gives 91.9 at the corpus's `damp` 70 and stays inside [0, 100] for every user `damp`; the curve by a gain `log2(3) / 1.5`), so no even byte moves and `env(500) = 1` holds since `uG(0) = 0` in every branch. Under `dampAmp` and `dampBias` the shoulder and side terms are untouched; the `--damp-amp` sweep in `tonal.mjs` reads them as before.

**Mechanism 3, READING (a) on the anchored path: the stop-300 ratio measures the L\* ladder, not the envelope (the U2 re-diagnosis, Question 1).** OKHSL `s` is gamut-relative: at a fixed `s`, CAM16 chroma scales with the gamut's width at that lightness. 1811 of 2853 anchored instances have an anchor L\* in [35, 50) and 271 below 35, so stop 500 (the anchor verbatim, ADR-026) sits well below the hue's cusp lightness while stop 300 (L\* 51 to 70) sits on it. The gate path has no such climb because it pivots stop 500 at the hue's own key lightness. **The coupling that sank #681 U3 twice: OKHSL `l` is a proxy for CIE L\* only at `s` = 0.** `tonal.js:1002` and `:1086` say it, #668 measured it, and the U3 re-diagnosis (Question 1) traced both reverted retunes (92/0.5, 98/0.65) to it: the envelope lowers `s` at a FIXED `l`, and the CIE L\* of the emitted colour moves with `s` by an amount that varies stop to stop with the damping slope; where two neighbouring stops receive noticeably different damping the measured L\* can rise across them (witnesses: BZZR Primary, perceptual, stop 500 to 550, 26.4403 to 26.6493 at 92/0.5; peak hue 110 lift 22, stop 450 to 500, 97.6484 to 97.7490 at 98/0.65). The re-diagnosis calls this Helmholtz-Kohlrausch; it is not (`tonal.js:691` to 693: H-K is perceived brightness and cannot move CIE L\*, a computed quantity). It is the OKHSL `l` to CIE L\* chroma coupling, and this plan names it that way in code and ADR. A steeper curve (`c` 1.585 against 1.5) concentrates slope, so the retune above WILL re-open it unless the tone is held; the two reverted attempts chose constants that happened to relocate where it fires, which is why a third plain retune is not attempted.

**The derived method for the uptick (requirement 3): hold the tone by solving `l`, per stop, for the pre-envelope CIE L\*.** Mechanism: the ramp's designed lightness is the CIE L\* of the pre-envelope colour `(hue, s_basis, l)` (`keyS` and `lightnessAt` on the non-anchored path; the capped `anchorChromaBasis` value and the `anchorLerp` `l` on the anchored path). The envelope is meant to move chroma only. So for each stop with `env < 1` take `targetL = lstarFromRgb(okhslToRgb(hue, s_basis, l))`, set `s = clamp(s_basis * env)`, and emit `(hue, s, l')` with `l' = solveLForTone(hue, s, targetL)` (the closure at `tonal.js:1209`, 30 halvings, or `okhslLAtChromatic` at 1008, the same bisection; L\* is monotone non-decreasing in `l` at fixed hue and `s` by OKHSL's construction, so it converges). Properties that follow, each a criterion below: `damp` 0 and stop 500 are the identity (env = 1, `l' = l`); the emitted L\* ladder equals the pre-envelope ladder to within the solve's tolerance, and on the non-anchored path that ladder is monotone by construction (fixed `s`, monotone `l`), so the uptick cannot fire there for ANY constants; on the anchored path `s_basis` varies per stop, so monotonicity is measured, not assumed (C3.4, C3.5). The peak joint cap composes after it unchanged (it reads `targetTone = lstarFromRgb(rgb)` of the tone-held colour). `enforceMonotonePixelL` (`tonal.js:691`) stays as the 8-bit rounding fixer only. This is the U3 re-diagnosis's Question 2 construction, built and measured.

**The hue the hold receives (revision 8, `chroma-envelope-U3-rediagnosis.md`).** Built at U3 pass 1, the hold on the anchored `hueSpace: "oklch"` path received the hue `solveOkhslHue` solved per stop at the damped `s`. OKHSL hue is OKLab hue by construction (`rgbToOklchHue(okhslToRgbFloat(h, s, l))` against `h` reads max 1.1e-13 degrees over 5,814 in-gamut points), so that solve was the identity in the continuous domain and its only effect was the 8-bit staircase its `#657` comment describes: at a damped pale stop every candidate renders the same pixel and the solve returns an arbitrary hue (106 against the anchor's 68.7 degrees at default Warning perceptual 150), which the hold then evaluates at the basis `s` where it is a visibly different colour (target 96.13 against 95.12 L*, the 1.007 x2 miss), and which differs per hueSpace so the peak cap's hue-blind, discrete polish lands the two on different 8-bit neighbours (3 codes at Info 350, 11 under a basis-`s` solve at Secondary 450). The construction that clears both: the anchored `oklch` hue IS the anchor's own OKLCH hue (`targetOklchHue`), no per-stop solve; `anchor.okhsl.h` on the `cam16` branch is the same number, so both hueSpaces are byte-identical on anchored perceptual and peak (what Q-D ruled and the pre-R3 measurement "0 of 3,396 moved" already showed). Measured on a scratch clone: x2 0 of 251,064 rows, f4 codes 0 and OKLab dE 0.0000 in both modes, hue fidelity mean 1.036 to 1.009 degrees (no measurable loss), the gate path and even untouched. R78 (no per-hue hard-coding; one general construction and one stated tolerance over hue- or key-specific constants) ranks it first among the six candidates: it removes a solver rather than adding one, and carries no constant, tolerance or key list of its own.
