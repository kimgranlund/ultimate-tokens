## Approach

Maison's geometry is not a ramp with a height knob; it is a fixed 25-row table (height to inset, text, icon in `geometry/component-geometry.csv`) addressed by three discrete axes. `definition.mjs` turns `(tier, scale, size)` into a height (`base + offsets[scale] + sizes[size] * step`), the table supplies the row, and a generator derives the compact row (caption and chip), the icon ratio, and four radius roles under four named modes. Everything in today's engine that assumes a continuous height retires with the knob: `baseHeight` and its `[20, 48]` clamp, the `baseHeight/28` factor, `rampContrast`, the five treatments, per-cell height `tokenOverrides` (an overridden height lands off-table), the `linear4` prototype, and `geomModeScales`' synthesized breakpoints with hand tables. I verified over the real CSV that `height === icon + 2 * inset` holds for all 25 rows (Maison asserts the same in its generator), so our centering law and its exact gate survive verbatim: Maison's `inset` is our `paddingNarrow`.

The new engine shape: `doc.geometry = { tier, scale, radius, spaceBase, modes?: [{ id, name, minWidth, scale }] }` resolving to `{ cells, cell (the default), radii, space, insets, gaps, borders, focus }`, where `cells` holds all 27 cells keyed `{tier}-{scale}-{size}` (in place of today's six `sizes`) with fields `height, inset, text, icon, captionText, chipHeight, chipInset, chipText, iconRatio, minWidth, radiusControl, radiusMark, radiusInset, radiusCard`. `orderedSizeNames`, `sizeAnchor`, and `mdAnchor` keep their signatures and now map the six legacy t-shirt names to cells (MD to `product-md-md`), so ds-export, the swatch board, the section, and the emitters never learn the cell naming. The container tier (`space`, `insets`, `gaps`, `borders`, `focus`) and the M3 `radii` ladder stay byte-identical: Maison keeps them as imported globals, and `exports.js` seeds shadcn/Panda/Radix/Tailwind from `radii.md`, which also keeps the pinned Adia artifacts stable.

Token names follow our grammar, tier in the path on every surface because DTCG and Figma have no selector: cell primitives `--{pfx}size-{tier}-{scale}-{size}-{field}` (the existing `size` family with a three-segment step, Figma `size/{cell}/{field}`), and a new resolved-role tier emitted only by CSS as a context resolver, Maison's own mechanism (0/1 indicator vars set by `:where([data-tier])`, `[data-scale]`, `[data-size]`, `[data-radius]`, a `calc` sum of products per role): `--g-height/-inset/-text/-icon/-caption-text/-icon-height-ratio` to `--control-height/-inset/-text/-icon/-caption-text/-icon-ratio`, `--g-chip-*` to `--chip-height/-inset/-text`, `--r-control/-mark/-inset/-card` to `--radius-control/-mark/-inset/-card`, `--m-scale-*/--m-size-*/--r-k-*` to `--ctx-scale-*/--ctx-size-*/--ctx-radius-text/-height`; `--g-micro-*` is scoped out (zero component consumers in Maison, and the micro tier's cells already carry the values). The attribute names and radius ids `default/round/sharp/pill` stay verbatim (config, not tokens; interop with Maison components). In Figma the shared "Geometry" collection keeps its breakpoint modes on both halves (`mode-apply-plan.mjs:45` requires matching mode lists): the 27 cells are mode-constant `size/{cell}/{field}`, and `control/{tier}/{size}/{field}` per mode aliases that mode's scale, the same primitives-to-roles cascade the color binder uses.

Type composition: today's UI-control voice `[12,13,15,16,18,20]` agrees with the CSV text column at only one of its six heights (48), and 27 cells over 15 distinct heights cannot be addressed by six steps. The `linear4` prototype resolved this by skipping composition, which the handoff forbids. The design moves the height-indexed Maison text column into `type.mjs` as the UI text table: UI-control at a cell's height, UI-widget at its compact row; the voices keep their character (weight, tracking, leading) and lose their fixed six-step rows; geometry composes `text` and `chipText` from it, value-neutral at defaults. Migration: `persist.js` RENAMES v8 maps `(treatment, baseHeight)` to the nearest `(tier, scale)` by md-cell height (so a default `comfortable/28` kit lands on `product/sm`, not the new default `product/md`), `modes[].baseHeight` to `modes[].scale`, and drops `rampContrast`, `ramp`, `tokenOverrides` through DROPPED_KEYS. Figma: a geometry wave in `migrations.mjs` maps the six legacy steps by height (XS `product-sm-sm`, SM `product-md-sm`, MD `product-sm-md`, LG `product-lg-md`, XL `content-md-md`, 2XL `content-lg-md`) and `size/{0..9}` by nearest height; 28 and 36 each match three cells, so `geometrySizeAliasMap`'s nearest-by-height needs one deterministic tiebreak (product > content > micro, then md > sm > lg); field map `padding-narrow` to `inset`, `font` to `text`, `pill-radius` to `radius-control`; caret, icon-gap, padding-wide and the compact pads deprecate id-preserving as `font` did in #498.

Validation of all 27 cells: vendor Maison's `geometryRows` (27) and the CSV (25) as `test/engine/fixtures/maison-geometry-rows.json` with the source sha256; `test/engine/geometry.mjs` deep-equals every cell, asserts the anatomy identity, the compact-row rule, and 27 x 4 = 108 radius values; the resolver cascade cannot run in the headless shim, so `test/smoke/smoke.mjs` renders the 108 nested attribute cases (Maison's own spec shape) and asserts `getComputedStyle` of `--control-height` and `--radius-control`; persist allowlist parity and the consumer `dimension-parity.mjs` re-target the cells.

### Glyphs, indicators, marks and their containers

Maison's model, read from its component styles (counts are `var(--g-*)` uses across `src/`): there is no caret, gap, or glyph-size token. Every glyph lives in one square box, `--g-icon` by `--g-icon` (417 uses, `foundation/icons/icons.css:17-20`, `control.mjs:17`); an icon fills the box (`font-size: --g-icon`), an indicator (caret, chevron, dot, count) sits in the same box at `font-size: --g-text` (`button.styles.css:140-141`, `select.styles.css:93-94`, `tag.styles.css:59-66`); the icon-to-label gap is `--g-inset / 2` everywhere (850 inset uses, no gap role); an icon-only control is `inline-size: --g-height`. Marks (`foundation/control/choice.mjs:21-22`): checkbox and radio boxes are `--g-icon` squares, checkbox corner `min(--r-mark, .3 * --g-icon)`, radio `50%` with a `--g-icon / 2` dot, switch track `1.75 * --g-icon` by `--g-icon` with thumb `--g-icon - 2 * edge` and corner `--r-mark` (25 uses: checkbox, switch, range, chart legend/tooltip). `--g-icon-height-ratio` is consumed only by the resolver to form `--r-mark`. Chips (`--g-chip-height/-inset/-text`, 72/15/79 uses) size kbd, tooltip, stepper markers, breadcrumb separators, inline code, OTP cells; a badge is `--g-height - --g-inset` tall with `--r-inset`; a tag is full height with `--r-inset`; a caption/note under a choice control is `--g-caption-text` (`note.mjs:4`, 55 uses). Components expose per-instance override hooks named `--control-height/-inset/-radius/-text` with the role as fallback (`control.mjs:10-13`).

Our model today: `buildSize` derives `icon = 2.49 * h^0.58`, `caret = 3.5 * h^0.39`, `gap = GAP_UNIT * factor * density`, four pads, `radiusPill`, `minWidth` per size (`geometry.mjs:210-228`); the Figma collection carries `icon`, `caret`, `icon-gap`, `pill-radius` per step (`GEOM_SIZE_FIELDS`, `:490`), `dsIconLayer` publishes `sizes.<step>.icon` as the icon library's render ramp (`ds-export.js:291-298`), the swatch board sizes thumbs from the LG icon (`png-swatch-board.mjs:195-198`), and the consumer skill teaches `--size-{step}-icon/-caret/-gap` (`geometry-tokens/references/detail.md`). Mapping: `icon` to `icon` (same name, CSV value); `caret` to nothing (an indicator is text-sized inside the icon box, so the planner records "caret = text" as the consumption rule, not a token); `gap` to nothing (`inset / 2` as a rule); `radiusPill` to `radiusMark`/`radiusControl` in pill mode; new fields `captionText`, `chipHeight/Inset/Text`, `iconRatio`, `radiusMark/Inset/Card`. Decisions: (G1) emit the glyph rules (`indicator = text`, `gap = inset / 2`, badge height = `height - inset`) as documentation plus two derived convenience tokens `--control-gap` and `--control-indicator`, or strictly Maison's role set (recommended: strict, the rules live in the consumer skill and the resolver comments, since Maison components prove the roles suffice). (G2) Keep `--g-icon-height-ratio` as an exported dimensionless token (`iconRatio`, recommended, Maison exports it and `--radius-mark` is defined through it) or fold it into `radiusMark` only. (G3) Name the resolved roles `--control-*`, which equals Maison's per-instance override hook names so Maison component CSS binds to our export with zero translation (recommended; the cost is that a local `--control-height` override and the role become one variable, which is what Maison's fallback chain does anyway), or pick a distinct family (`--geo-*`). (G4) Carry chip and caption roles into Figma as per-cell variables (recommended, they are plain numbers) or CSS only.

### The app shell and its components

The `<ultimate-tokens>` shell is fixed-px today: `src/ui/styles.css` has 954 px literals and 24 custom properties, of which exactly one is a control dimension (`--ctl-thumb: 15px`, `:51`, with a comment deferring the size ramp to "a visual pass"); the shell radii are its own `--r-sm/--r/--r-lg` (5/8/12, `:46-48`), the chrome heights `--hh/--ch/--fh` (48/42/30), buttons and inputs carry literal padding and `font-size: 13px` (`:68`, `:157-201`), chips `padding: 3px 9px; font-size: 11px` (`:791-794`), the switch `34x19` (`:958`), and `icons.js` `icon(name, { size = 16 })` writes the SVG width/height from a per-call literal (12, 13, 16 across the sections). There is no density mechanism, and the only data attribute the shell reads is `data-theme` (`:75-77`). Design: the host sets `data-tier`, `data-scale`, `data-size`, `data-radius` from `doc.geometry` (default `product/md/md/round`), with a Settings override so the user can preview a kit in the shell without resizing their editor; `styles.css` declares one alias block, `--sh-control-height/-inset/-text/-icon/-radius: var(--control-height ...)`, and selector groups move to the aliases one at a time (buttons and inputs, then segmented and mode controls, then chips and switch, then icons via `icon()` reading `--control-icon` with its literal as fallback). Migration path: incremental by selector group, each group's screenshot approved, is recommended over one cut, because 954 literals with hand-tuned sub-pixel values (9.5px, 10.5px, 11.5px) will not survive a mechanical replacement and the shell's chrome heights (`--hh/--ch/--fh`) are layout, not control geometry, and should stay literal. Risk to tests: the headless shim asserts attributes, class names and DOM shape, never computed px, so a CSS-only migration keeps it green by construction (`a37`); `test/smoke/smoke.mjs` has 18 geometry-sensitive probes (`getBoundingClientRect`, `offsetHeight`, exact px drop sensitivity at `:138-149`, drawer height at `:157`) that will move when buttons and handles change size, so each selector group needs its smoke probes re-baselined in the same step. Decisions: (S1) ride with the engine change or split to its own ticket (recommended: split; the engine change is already XL and the shell has no parity gate, only screenshots); (S2) default shell cell `product/md/md` at 32px (the shell's buttons are about 28px today, so the shell grows) or `product/sm/md` at 28px to hold today's pixels (recommended: `product/sm` for the shell's own chrome, the kit default for the preview canvas, so the editor does not change size when the user changes their kit); (S3) whether the shell's own `--r-sm/--r/--r-lg` alias to `--radius-inset/-control/-card` or stay literal.

Decisions the user must make for the engine change (v2 manifest encodes the recommendation; the alternative moves the named nodes to a later manifest): (1) UI voices become height-indexed over the Maison text column (n2, a30) vs keep six steps re-ratified to the CSV at 20/24/28/36/48/64 and compose by nearest height. (2) Retire treatment/baseHeight/rampContrast/tokenOverrides (n1c, n3) vs keep per-cell overrides snapped to the nearest CSV row. (3) Retire `linear4` and remove `geometry.ramp` from the Adia preset in `brands.json` (a28) vs keep it as a third ramp id. (4) New-doc default `product/md/round` with existing kits migrating by nearest height. (5) Figma: 27 mode-constant cells plus 9 per-mode aliases, about 27 x 14 + 9 x 14 variables against today's 60 per mode, vs only each mode's 9 cells. (6) Keep `min-width`; retire caret, icon-gap, density, padding-wide, the compact pads, pill-radius and `.control-{step}` (a29), each with a migration note. (7) Attribute names and radius ids verbatim from Maison. Plus G1 to G4 and S1 to S3 above.

## Rejected alternatives

- Skip composition while the ladder is active (what `linear4` did, `geometry.mjs:116-120`): the handoff names composition a project invariant.
- Keep the six t-shirt names as aliases over cells: 28 and 36 are three-way ambiguous and the tier axis disappears from the token.
- Tier as a CSS selector only with `--cell-{scale}-{size}` paths (Maison's literal shape): DTCG and Figma have no selector, so tier must sit in the path on every surface.
- A resolver-only CSS export without the 27 primitives in DTCG/Figma: fails the done-criterion that every export reflects the ladder.
- Keep `baseHeight` and snap to the nearest CSV row: a continuous knob over a discrete standard reintroduces the drift Maison's "validate all 27 cells" rule exists to prevent.
- Replace the M3 radius ladder with Maison's control radius: `exports.js` and the pinned Adia artifacts seed from `radii.md`; Maison keeps the ladder as an import too.
- Keep a `caret` token sized by our power law beside Maison's roles: Maison components never read one, so it would be a token with no consumer and a second anatomy to validate.
- One-cut shell migration (replace every px literal in `styles.css`): hand-tuned sub-pixel values and the layout chrome heights are not control geometry; a mechanical pass would change the editor's pixels everywhere at once with only screenshots as the gate.

## Risks

- Decision (1) is the largest blast radius: `type.mjs` (`RANKS6`, box-voice `singleLineHeight`, `typeTokensFigmaModes`), the typography section, the type-scale and typography-tokens skills; the planner should run it as its own serial slice, and if the user picks the alternative the composition stays lossy at every height but 48.
- Users with saved `tokenOverrides` lose those edits (reported, not preserved); treatments' density and gap feel disappears with no replacement token.
- Retiring `caret` and `icon-gap` changes `dsIconLayer`, the swatch board, and the consumer skill's four-pad doctrine at once; a Figma file bound to `size/{step}/caret` keeps the variable by id but it stops updating.
- The `control/{tier}/{size}` aliases make the Figma collection roughly six times larger; apply time and the binder's reconcile loop are untested at that size.
- `geomModeScales`' standard set maps breakpoints onto only two scales (sm and lg beside md), so a kit author who wanted the old -2/-4/+4/+28 px shifts gets +-4 or +-8 instead.
- Naming the roles `--control-*` (G3) means a Maison component's per-instance override hook and our role share a variable; if Maison later diverges the two, our export binds to the wrong level.
- Shell adoption at `product/md` (S2) grows every editor control by about 4px; at `product/sm` the shell and the kit preview differ in cell, which the Geometry canvas must make visible or users will read the chrome as the kit.
- The Maison repo is external: if `definition.mjs`, the CSV, or the component conventions (indicator = text, gap = inset / 2) change, the vendored fixture and the consumer skill pin stale rules until someone re-vendors.
- If the new ADR number is claimed by a sibling architect this session, the accept greps the title, not the number.
