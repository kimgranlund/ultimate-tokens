## Task goal
Make the chroma envelope's closed-form curve the spec: a few named preset curves (each a damp, dampCurve, shoulder triple, knobs still exposed), and gates that assert the continuous curve within one stated tolerance instead of pinning rounded 8-bit pixel values. Full issue: `gh issue view 778`.

- `src/engine/tonal.js`, stop records. Every record gains `env` (the `chromaEnvelope` value used at that stop) and `model` (the continuous chroma before 8-bit quantization, in the path's own unit). Per site:
  - `okhslStops`: `env: envelopeAt.get(stop)`, `model: hold.s` (the pre-cap value, not the `s` that `capChromaAtHeldTone` reassigns).
  - `okhslStopsAnchored`: `preCap` also returns `env`; the record takes `env` and `model: s` (preCap's `hold.s`; the cap does not reassign `s` there). The verbatim stop-500 record: `env: 1`, `model: anchor.okhsl.s`.
  - Even, both paths (`paletteStopsAnchored`, `paletteStops`): `model: chroma` (the CAM16 C passed to `hctToRgb`), plus `basis` (the un-damped intended chroma fed to `evenChroma` at the stop's final hue) and `floor` (`evenChroma`'s `floorC` there). The anchored verbatim stop 500: `env: 1`, `model: anchor.cam.chroma`, `basis: anchor.cam.chroma`, `floor: 0`. Non-anchored at `dampAmp === 0`: also `anchorCap: anchorChroma` (the stop-500 cap at `tonal.js:1040`); absent otherwise. To keep the arithmetic identical, move `evenChroma`'s floor expression into a helper that `evenChroma` itself calls, and on the anchored path make `chromaAt` return the parts (`chroma`, `basis`, `floor`) through a wrapper. `solveCam16Hue` must still receive a number.
  - `enforceMonotonePixelL`: a swapped stop gets `refined: true`, and its `model`, `env`, `basis` and `floor` stay as built.
  - `dampStops`: every record it returns gets `damper: r`. The OKHSL branch sets `model: st.model * r`. The even branch sets `model: Math.min(st.model * r, out.maxc)` after `out.maxc` is set. The architect missed this path, but 16 of the 2,920 envelope instances render below chroma 100 through it (plan-time probe).
  - Keep the `chromaEnvelope(` count at 5 (C7, `test/engine/tonal.mjs:1308-1313`). Keep the text `chromaEnvelope(stop, 500, lift, controls)` (the C7 negative-control target, `tonal.mjs:1325`) and `let rgb = okhslToRgb(hue, s, l1);` (step 2's planted-regression target) verbatim.
  - Evidence that the fields are byte-neutral: a plan-time scratch copy of HEAD's `tonal.js` with `env`/`model`/`refined`/`damper` added at these sites left `--identity-control` at 0 differing cells. `npm test` (168 s) and `npm run gate:sweeps` (870 s) were both green on it.
