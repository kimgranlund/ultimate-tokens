## Task goal
A kit can opt in to a "match peer lightness" mode in which every enabled palette's ramp (and so the Radix export and 53 roles) lands at the same CIELAB lightness per stop across peer palettes, even when palettes are anchored or carry skew or lift. Default behaviour and every stored document and preset stay byte-identical.

With the flag on, the tone edge moves to 500 on both sides: every stop's tone is `sharedToneAt(stop)`. Settled against the architect's single `bandEdge` parameter, on the handoff's "the anchor hue and chroma still hold": the chroma rule keeps step 2's shape (the end tint at stops at or below 100 and at or above 900, the blend between, the palette's own chroma basis at 500), so only the tone edge moves. Stop 500 is no longer the anchor pixel: it is built like the clamped-pivot case (`:881`, `:1418`) at the anchor's hue, held in the document's hue space (ADR-031), and the anchor's chroma basis, gamut-clamped at the shared tone. Skew and lift stop moving lightness (lift keeps its chroma-envelope role); vibrancy and cusp pull read `t = 0`; peak keeps only its chroma cap (ceiling from `preCap(500).chroma`, `:1471`). Mode off renders step 3's tree byte for byte.
