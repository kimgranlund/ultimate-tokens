Both lists describe the same construction. After #681 U3's pass 7, the even envelope is `1 - damp' * |sd|^(0.375)` around the anchor (`EVEN_DAMP_FACTOR` 0.25 scales both `damp`'s headroom and `dampCurve`), and the floor is `chromaFloor% * maxc(stop)`, gamut-relative at every stop. Two artifacts follow:

1. The envelope exponent is under 1, so the envelope has infinite slope at the anchor: one stop away (`|sd|` = 50/450) it is already 0.594 at the shipped `damp` 70. With U2's pass-through pinning stop 500 to the anchor's full chroma and U3's `dampAmp` 0 removing the only term that used to prop the shoulders, stops 450 and 550 render at about 60% of stop 500 in CAM16 C, and for muted anchors that lands them under the gate's 0.05 OKLCH bound while 500 sits at 0.07 to 0.09. That is the 64 lone spikes (U4 review pass 1 §1, root-caused by a single-variable swap: the same engine with `dampAmp` 55 reads 0).
2. The floor is shaped by `maxc(stop)`, whose hue-cusp-driven curve is not monotone, so in a narrow band the floor has passed its local peak while the damped value has not caught up: a local minimum of 3 to 4 CAM16 C. That is the 53 named dips, measured on U3's branch at stops 350 and 650.

## Constraints every unit holds (the color-math invariants, restated for this change)

- `chromaEnvelope` stays one exported function, called from the even path's two sites and the OKHSL path (C7's greps). The even-only branch is the only place a term may be added; perceptual and peak are byte-identical before and after (C6).
- `env(anchorStop) = 1` exactly for every controls combination and every lift (`env(500) = 1` sweep in the report). A shoulder term is a factor on the off-anchor part, never an offset.
- In-gamut by construction: `chroma = min(maxc, max(damped, floorC))` keeps its shape; the floor never exceeds `intended` (a muted palette stays muted, a chroma-0 neutral stays untinted, `chroma-floor` (b) and (c)).
- Damping touches chroma only; `toneAt` sets CIELAB L* on the even path directly, so no even change can move measured L* (the reason #681 revision 22 put the even retune first). The uptick and duplicate-hex gates stay at 0 anyway (C7).
- Both sliders stay live in even mode (`damp`, `dampCurve`, `chromaFloor`): F4, no dead control, no new control. Any new constant is derived from a slider or named and documented.
- Generated palettes (`dampAmp` 0) never emit more chroma than the anchor in even mode (the `Math.min(chroma, anchorChroma)` cap in `paletteStops`, C6's "0 above 100%"). A shoulder that lifts 450/550 toward 1 must respect it on the anchored path too, where no cap exists today (the probe counts 14,979 above-anchor stops there before and after at r = 0.2, all from `anchorChromaBasis` blending toward a group target above the anchor's own chroma; not this plan's, reported under C5).
