# ADR-025 · WCAG-safe on-colors are the DEFAULT, with an achromatic fall-through · 2026-09-18 · DECIDED
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Decision | `onColorMode` defaults to `"contrast"` (`tonal.js` DEFAULT_CONTROLS and `persist.js` DOMAINS, which must agree or a stored kit hydrates onto the other policy), and the policy gains an achromatic fall-through: a ramp end is kept while it clears AA 4.5:1, and otherwise the on-color takes the pure `white`/`black` constant with the better contrast. `on{N}Variant` follows the side the prime chose. `"fixed"` remains as the opt-out. |
| Forces | ADR-003 pinned `on{N}` to `050` with `onColorMode: "contrast"` only as an opt-in, so the default document shipped accents missing WCAG AA against their own on-color (perceptual dark Secondary 3.05; peak Secondary 1.24), and the Adia document's Park `solid.fg` on `solid.bg` missed 18 of 32 cells. Flipping the default alone still left six default cells and two Adia cells under 4.5, because neither ramp end clears it against a mid-lightness accent. |
| Rejected | flipping the default alone (measured: six default cells and two Adia cells still miss); retuning each family's skew and lift (would move the ramps and change the product's colors, against the owner's constraint that no ramp stop moves) |
| Consequences | All 16 default families clear AA 4.5:1 in both schemes in all three tone modes (96 of 96 cells, from 52); the Adia Park mapping clears all 32, from 14. On-color refs may now be `white`/`black`, routed to the constants namespace. Accent on-colors moved in every export format; no ramp stop moved. OD-001 is CLOSED. |
| Supersedes / amended by | Amends ADR-003 (its default no longer ships; its decision stands as the record); closes OD-001; `hpg-role-contrast` gate in `test/engine/semantic.mjs` |
