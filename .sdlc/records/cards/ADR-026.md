# ADR-026 · A palette's anchor is STORED, not fitted: the sampled source colour is the record · 2026-09-20 · DECIDED
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Decision | The source hex is stored on the palette as `anchor`, and the engine reads it rather than re-deriving it: a palette with a valid `anchor` emits it verbatim at `prime.DEFAULT` and, when the source sits inside the ramp window `[9.95, 95.05]` L\*, at ramp stop 500 only at group 100 (R94: a group value below 100 damps the whole ramp, stop 500 included, in perceptual, peak and even alike). `sourceAnchor` is the generator's own copy, written only by `scripts/gen-categories.mjs` and `defaultDocument()`. Editing `hue` or `chroma` detaches the palette (`anchor` removed, `sourceAnchor` kept for Reset); `skew` and `lift` never detach and never move the anchor. |
| Forces | A curated preset kept only `{hue, chroma, skew, lift}` fitted to its sampled colour, so the key colour the engine re-derived was a reconstruction: before #681, `prime.DEFAULT` matched the source byte for byte for essentially none of the 3,380 sampled palettes, worst stop-500 lightness error around 23 L\*. |
| Rejected | fitting harder: four parameters cannot in general reproduce an arbitrary sRGB colour through a cusp-derived key colour, so the residual is structural |
| Consequences | `prime.DEFAULT` moved for all 3,380 fitted palettes and the 16 default families, so every colour export moved once. Ten sources outside the ramp window keep an exact token while stop 500 lands at the window edge; the ramp and ladder allow-lists are frozen by name and count. The meaning of "percentage of stop 500" changes: C6's median and p90 chroma bars miss in 14 of 24 checks, recorded not resolved, owned by #725 (perceptual and peak) and #701 (even-mode `chromaFloor`); the owner accepted closing #681 with #725 open. |
