## Approach

The rule is one sentence, applied to every anchored surface: the hue space names the space in which the anchor's own measured hue is held constant across a surface, `oklch` holds the anchor's OKLCH hue, `cam16` holds its CAM16 hue, and the anchor pixel itself (stop 500, rung 3, the key tile at Prime chroma k 100) is verbatim in both spaces because every constancy line passes through it by construction. The even-mode anchored ramp already implements both branches (`paletteStopsAnchored`, `src/engine/tonal.js:851-958`: `cam16` holds `anchor.cam.hue`, `oklch` solves per stop with `solveCam16Hue`), so it is the model. Today's other anchored surfaces each carry only one branch, asymmetrically: the perceptual/peak ramp holds the anchor's OKLCH hue in both spaces (`okhslStopsAnchored`, `tonal.js:1401`, the Q-D ruling "made structural"), while the prime ladder (`src/engine/prime.mjs:155`, `hOk = cam.hue`, held through `hctToRgb` on every rung) and `deriveKeyColor` at k != 1 (`src/ui/model.mjs:879-883`) hold the anchor's CAM16 hue in both spaces. T-0015 adds the missing branch to each surface, no new hue model: (1) `okhslStopsAnchored` gets a `cam16` branch that solves per stop, jointly with `holdTone`, the OKHSL hue whose float render (`okhslToRgbFloat`, no 8-bit staircase) carries `anchor.cam.hue`, a bracketed root-find mirroring `solveCam16Hue`'s `chromaAt` form (`tonal.js:236-263`), keeping the anchor's OKLCH hue when no root exists or the candidate is achromatic (the even path's own guard, `tonal.js:199-210`); `capChromaAtHeldTone` receives the anchor's CAM16 hue plus shift instead of `null` under `cam16` (`tonal.js:1411-1416`); the `oklch` branch stays byte-identical. (2) `primeSwatches` gets an `oklch` branch: a shared `rungHue(i, l)` used by both `rungHex` and the final map solves the CAM16 hue whose render at `min(cPrime, maxChromaInGamut(h, l))` and the rung's L carries the anchor's OKLCH hue (`solveCam16Hue` with `chromaAt`, exported from `tonal.js`, prime.mjs already imports `effHue` from there); rung 3 at k != 1 solves the same way under `oklch`, at k 1 it is the stored bytes in both spaces; the `cam16` branch stays byte-identical. (3) `deriveKeyColor` anchored k != 1 mirrors rung 3's arithmetic with its own call, never via `primeSwatches`, so `key-anchor`/`anchor-k` keep two producers. Achromatic anchors (`anchor.achromatic`, #739) already route through `effHue(palette.hue, hueSpace, ...)` in both anchored ramp paths and stay untouched; the ladder's near-zero `cPrime` makes its hue moot there.

Measured against the T-0014 worktree (scratch scripts, estimate holding each stop's OKHSL s and l fixed while re-solving its hue): the ladder half is visible and large, 3,128 of 3,380 corpus anchored ladders change at least one outer-rung hex at the DEFAULT space (oklch, k 100), max OKLab dE 0.037; default kit 16 anchored, outer-rung OKLCH drift today 0.4 to 8.0 degrees (Primary 5.5, Data 1 8.0). The perceptual/peak half is physically sub-JND: CAM16 constancy and OKLCH constancy differ by at most 0.014 OKLab dE anywhere in the corpus (2,835 of 3,380 ramps move by at least one hex, 1 clears 0.01; a full-circle solve swings 1,574 pale stops more than 30 degrees of OKHSL hue at saturations where that is invisible, and 3,210 pale stop-solves found no bracketed root at a 5-degree scan, the CAM16 hue there is ill-conditioned in the OKHSL hue, so any solve is noise). This re-confirms the Q-D bound with vibrancy 50 (its 0.0047/0.0053 becomes about 0.014) and means no continuous construction that keeps the anchor at stop 500 can make an anchored perceptual ramp move "visibly" under the toggle; the construction that would (re-seeding the ramp hue from the anchor's CAM16 hue at the cusp, as the non-anchored `okhslStops` does) puts a hue kink at stop 500 and is rejected below. Even ramps already clear the F4 magnitude floor (1,060 of 3,396 ramps clear 0.01 OKLab dE, below the about 0.02 JND; no even-mode maximum measured here).

## Rejected alternatives

- Reinterpret the anchor's hue number across spaces (treat its CAM16 hue as an OKLCH hue or the reverse): the anchor is a pixel, not a slider value; there is no ambiguous number to reinterpret.
- Re-seed the anchored perceptual ramp hue from the anchor's CAM16 hue at the cusp (the non-anchored `okhslStops` cam16 branch, `tonal.js:1485`): makes the toggle visibly move the ramp, but stop 500 stays verbatim so the ramp's hue line no longer passes through the anchor, a hue kink at 500 (the discontinuity class Q3 (b) and C5 exist to prevent).
- One-shot solve at the anchor's own point: degenerate, always returns the anchor's own hue (R3's measured lesson, `tonal.js:856-860`).
- Hold the ladder on CAM16 in both spaces and only enable the UI: the toggle would be dead for the ladder, failing the done-when, and the ramp half is sub-JND, so nothing would move.
- Schema v9 for a semantics-only change: no field, rename or translation exists; the convention bumps for schema-affecting changes only, and a stamp cannot tell a chosen `cam16` from a legacy-stamped one.
- Solve on 8-bit rounded renders: the #725 revision 8 pathology (arbitrary hues at pale damped stops read off the staircase); the float domain removes it.
- A dE > 0.01 liveness floor for perceptual/peak anchored ramps: the corpus maximum is about 0.014 and most ramps sit far below; such a floor would red on the default kit or assert noise.

## Risks

- The user's stated expectation (anchored perceptual ramps move visibly) is not satisfiable under any continuous, anchor-preserving construction; if decision 1 goes to alternative A the design must change (kink) and C5/Q3 (b) rulings reopen.
- The joint `holdTone` solve may expose edge cases the fixed-(s,l) estimate did not (ill-conditioned pale stops are common, 3,210 stop-solves); the guard must keep the OKLCH hue there or pale stops invent tints.
- The ladder movement at the default space is large by count (3,128 of 3,380) and reaches exported `prime.*` tokens and the Figma binder's values; without decision 2 and the before/after report "done" is undefined.
- `test/ui/shell.mjs` oklch-native bar and any `anchor-ladder` dupe/order allow-lists (`RAMP_*_ALLOW`, `NOTCH_ALLOW` are ramp-side; the ladder's own distinctness search may re-freeze) need re-pinning after measurement.
- ADR number and the shared `decision-records.md`/`prime.mjs`/`model.mjs` lines depend on T-0014 landing first; reversed order forces a merge of two edits in the same anchored branches.
- Corpus sweep runtime grows: perceptual/peak anchored ramps under cam16 add a per-stop solve, but the default space and the corpus (all oklch) do not take that branch, so `gate:sweeps` cost moves mainly through the ladder's six per-palette solves.
- Legacy cam16-stamped user documents change render with no migration and no in-repo sample to measure.
